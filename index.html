What It Is

A web-based dashboard that visualizes and explores digital economy solutions submitted to the 2025 Global Call for Solutions. It is designed to help users discover, filter and explore the information applicants provided.

🛠️

What It Does

The tool provides:

  • Interactive Map Visualization: Shows solutions by country of origin with geographic distribution
  • Multi-dimensional Filtering: Filter by region, country, organization type, maturity stage, and SDG focus
  • Data Analytics:
    • SDG (Sustainable Development Goals) focus as reported by applicants
    • Organization type distribution charts
    • Solution scoring and rating system (1-5 stars)
  • Solution Discovery: Browse and explore solutions with detailed information
  • Mobile-Responsive Design: Works across all device sizes
  • AI-Powered Chatbot: Provides navigation support. Its responses are informational and do not constitute UN certification, endorsement or due-diligence clearance of any solution, score, rating or award
🌍

Why It Matters

This tool addresses several needs:

  • Solution Visibility: Makes submitted digital solutions discoverable and accessible globally
  • Rating Overview: Presents the six-criteria assessment of the information submitted for each solution, not an independent verification of reported impact
  • Knowledge Sharing: Facilitates learning and collaboration across regions and organizations
  • SDG Focus: Shows the SDGs applicants associated with their solutions
  • Initial Discovery: Helps policymakers, researchers and organizations identify solutions they may wish to assess further

🎯 Assessment Criteria

The six dimensions and point allocations used in the 2025 assessment are listed below. They describe the information submitted, not verified outcomes.

🎯 Relevance & Problem Alignment (25 pts)

Definition: How well the solution addresses a clearly identified and significant problem aligned with global challenges.

Evaluation Areas: Problem clarity (Q10–Q12), solution overview (Q13), thematic alignment (Q15).

💡 Innovation & Differentiation (15 pts)

Definition: The degree of novelty, creativity, and differentiation from existing solutions.

Evaluation Areas: Unique features (Q24), emerging tech (Q23), innovative delivery models (Q13).

🌍 Impact & Sustainability (25 pts)

Definition: The expected scale and depth of positive change, with potential for long-term, systemic, and self-sustaining results.

Evaluation Areas: Expected impact (Q22), SDG contribution (Q21), sustainability model (Q25).

⚡ Feasibility & Scalability (15 pts)

Definition: The practicality of implementation given resources, context, and readiness, and the ability to expand into new geographies.

Evaluation Areas: Maturity stage (Q17), adaptability (Q16), track record (Q18–Q19), pilot timeline (Q20).

🤝 Partnerships & Support (10 pts)

Definition: The strength of organizational capacity, networks, and stakeholder engagement.

Evaluation Areas: Provider credibility (Q14), endorsements/testimonials (Q28).

💰 Resource Strategy (10 pts)

Definition: The availability and diversity of resources to sustain and grow the solution.

Evaluation Areas: Funding sources (Q27), cost-effectiveness.

The six criteria and point allocations above describe the assessment of the information submitted to the 2025 Global Call. Scores and ratings summarize that assessment; they are not an independent verification of reported results. Read the full disclaimer.

📊 Understanding the Dashboard

In this dashboard, you can:

  • Filter the field: Use the region, country, organization type, maturity stage and SDG filters to narrow the solutions shown
  • Explore the field: See how solutions are distributed across countries, regions and organization types
  • Identify patterns: See which thematic areas and maturity stages are represented
  • Read the ratings: See how the assessment bands are distributed across the solutions

Ratings describe the assessment of submitted information, not verified performance. The full disclaimer sets out the scope of the information, the assessment and any recognition.
